# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

We want this project to be a place where people can contribute knowledge about a
stressful, confusing subject without being belittled for what they do not know.
Everyone participating in this project is expected to help make that true.

## Expected behaviour

- Be direct about substance and considerate about people.
- Assume good faith about mistakes, including legal ones. Someone
  misunderstanding a rule is an opportunity to source it properly.
- Say when you are uncertain. "I think, but I have not verified" is welcome
  here; false confidence is not.
- Give credit for others' research.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks; discrimination or demeaning comments
  about anyone's identity or circumstances.
- Publishing another person's private information, including tax affairs,
  wallet addresses or identity linkages, without permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, or persistent unsolicited contact.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention.

## Specific to this project

- **Do not share other people's financial data**, including in bug reports or
  screenshots. Reproduce with synthetic fixtures.
- **Do not present yourself as someone's tax adviser** in project spaces, or
  offer individual advice as if this project were an advisory relationship. You
  may share sourced general information.
- **Do not use the project to accuse identifiable people of tax offences.** The
  public case database records public information about decided or reported
  matters; it is not a place to build a case against someone.
- **Do not pressure a contributor to overstate certainty** so an answer looks
  cleaner. Understating uncertainty causes real harm to real people.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces — the repository, issues, pull requests and
any other channel — and when representing the project in public.

## Reporting

Report a concern privately to the maintainers via GitHub. Reports will be
handled discreetly, and the person reporting will not be required to confront
the other party.

Maintainers who do not follow or enforce this code in good faith may be removed
from the project.

## Enforcement

Depending on severity: a private word, a public correction, a temporary
restriction, or a permanent ban. Maintainers will explain the decision to the
person affected.
