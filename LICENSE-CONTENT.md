# Content license

Original prose authored in this repository (human-readable explanations,
planning documents, scenario narratives) is licensed under
[CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

Official statutes, BOFiP, administrative guidance, and other third-party
legal texts are **not** covered. Store metadata, links, hashes, archive
references, and only those extracts the `redistribution` field allows.
Where Licence Ouverte (or equivalent) permits a fuller mirror, record that
on the source and prefer the mirror over a rotting URL.
